Questions men ask before booking.

Do I need to spend a lot on new clothes?

No. You set the budget. The diagnostic starts with what you already own, and many fixes are tailoring rather than buying.

What happens on the free fit call?

Thirty minutes on WhatsApp or a call. We ask about your work, your weekends and what frustrates you about getting dressed, then suggest where to start.

What happens in the frame read?

A 60-minute session in person. We assess your build, proportions and colouring, and give you three fit fixes you can act on that week.

Does this work for my build?

Yes. The method starts with your frame, whether you are slim, broad, tall or shorter. That is the point of not copying trends.

What if I do not like a recommendation?

Tell us. The blueprint is reviewed with you before it is final, and your taste is part of the brief.

Do I have to wear linen and sandals?

No. The lookbook shows one man's wardrobe. Yours is built around your job, your taste and how formal your week is.

An engagement runs in four steps, usually over three to six weeks.

  1. 1Fit call

    A free 30-minute call about your week, your work and what frustrates you about getting dressed.

  2. 2Read the frame

    In person, we assess build, proportion and colouring, and review what you already own.

  3. 3Blueprint
